# Ender Essentials Minecraft Mod

Ender Essentials is a comprehensive Minecraft mod that brings together all the essentials for a smooth, adventurous, and high-performance experience. It introduces convenience features like infinite hunger, infinite durability, and no fall damage, alongside practical tools such as waypoints, pets, and procedural animations. The mod also enhances adventure with big melee weapons, dangerous mobs, plenty of generated structures, and a performance boost for better FPS.

## Install

1. Install **Fabric Loader** (version 0.15.0 or higher) for Minecraft 1.20.1.
2. Download and install **Fabric API** (version 0.92.2+1.20.1) into your `mods` folder.
3. Download the `ender-essentials-1.0.0.jar` file and place it into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game with the Fabric profile and create or open a world.

## Use

Once installed, Ender Essentials automatically applies its convenience features and adds new content to your world. Here are the key things you can do:

- **Convenience**: Hunger, durability, and fall damage are handled automatically—no need to eat, repair tools, or worry about falling.
- **Waypoints**: Use commands to manage waypoints:
  - `/waypoint add <name>` – save your current location.
  - `/waypoint teleport <name>` – teleport to a saved waypoint.
  - `/waypoint list` – view all saved waypoints.
  - `/waypoint gui` – open the graphical waypoint interface.
  - Waypoints appear on your HUD and as glowing markers in the world.
- **Pets**: Tame Pet Wolves with bones or spawn one instantly with `/mod pet spawn`. They follow and protect you.
- **Weapons**: Get powerful big melee weapons using `/mod give greatsword`, `/mod give cleaver`, `/mod give warhammer`, or `/mod give longsword`.
- **Mobs**: Encounter the dangerous Warrior Beast in dark and nether biomes.
- **Structures**: Explore 10 new procedural structures, including the Ancient Dragon Den, Crystal Ruins, Sky Temple, and more.
- **Performance**: Enjoy a built-in performance boost for smoother gameplay.
